A tiny ritual for everyday progress

Whether you're clearing space before a move or just want to enjoy what you have, Hitpan makes each use count.

1

Put it on your shelf

Add an item, choose how to track it, and set a goal date if you have one.

2

Log a little use

Record each use and see your progress over time. You can use the same item more than once a day.

3

Hit pan!

Finish something, celebrate the moment, and keep it in your archive.

For your real life, and your real shelf.

Track the things that tend to gather around your home, from a pump bottle in the bathroom to a box in the pantry.

  • See your items together in a cozy room.
  • Track by weight, volume, or an estimated amount used.
  • Keep personal goals and notes alongside your progress.
